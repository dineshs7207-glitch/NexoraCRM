Nexora privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Nexora is a private sales and CRM workspace used by our own team. Only people an administrator invites can sign in.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, and, if you choose "Continue with Google", the basic profile Google shares (name, email and profile picture). We don't ask Google for anything else.
  • The work records you and your teammates enter: leads, accounts, contacts, deals, quotes, invoices, tasks, projects and notes.
  • Sign-in records and an audit log of changes, kept for security.

How we use it

Only to run the workspace: to sign you in, show you the records your role allows, and keep a history of changes. We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone outside the team.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider, and protected so each person only sees the records their role allows. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Your choices

You can ask an administrator to correct your details or remove your account at any time. If you signed in with Google, you can also revoke Nexora's access from your Google Account's security settings.
